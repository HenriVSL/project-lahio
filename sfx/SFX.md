# KAUPUNKI sound pack (T07)

All files are generated by `tools/make_sfx.py` (Python 3 standard library only, deterministic: rerunning gives
identical bytes). Format: PCM WAV, 22050 Hz, 16-bit, mono. Effects are peak-normalised to -3 dBFS with 4 ms fades
(start/end at silence); ambient loops are seamless and normalised to -6 dBFS. Relative loudness in the game comes
from the base volume table in `src/snd.cpp` (`s_info`), not from the files.

## Regenerate

```sh
python3 tools/make_sfx.py --report        # all files, prints peak/RMS/clipping and loop-seam checks (exit 1 on failure)
python3 tools/make_sfx.py --only chime    # one file
```
Checks per file: no clipped samples, peak > -20 dBFS, one-shots start and end silent; loops: the jump from the last
to the first sample is within the 99th percentile of ordinary sample steps, and the RMS of the last and first 100 ms
differ by < 3 dB. In-game check: `lahio.exe -window -test snd -log` (needs the VM lock) loads every file, creates
its buffer, plays each effect once, crossfades the ambient layers and logs everything to `lahio.log`.

## Ambient loops (`AMB_*`)

| id | file | sec | base | sound | suggested driver (game.cpp) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AMB_BIRDS | amb_birds.wav | 8.0 | 70 | wind in trees, finches, blackbird warble | high in a small town; parks/forest on screen; lower as the town grows |
| AMB_TRAFFIC | amb_traffic.wav | 6.0 | 70 | low rumble + soft distant car passes | population / road traffic on screen |
| AMB_INDUSTRY | amb_industry.wav | 6.0 | 55 | motor hum, band-saw whine swelling twice per loop, clanks | industry lots on/near screen |

`snd_ambient(layer, 0..100)` (or `snd_set_ambient(b, t, i)`) sets a target; `snd_tick` glides each layer there in
1.5 s for the full range, and a layer at 0 is stopped (no mixing cost).

## Volumes

final = file base * caller vol100 * master (`volume`) * group, where the groups come from LAHIO.INI
`[Lahio] sfx_volume` (default 100) and `ambient_volume` (default 70), read by `snd_init` and settable with
`snd_set_group_volume`. Positional effects (`snd_play_at`) are full volume within 8 tiles of the camera centre and
silent from 48; pan is +-20 dB at pan100 = +-100; `snd_play_sx` uses a gentle +-60% pan from the screen x.

Size: about 1.37 MB in total (effects 0.48 MB, loops 0.88 MB), all held in DirectSound static buffers.
